# Product and career improvements
Four video themes reviewed in the conversation: cybersecurity career map, vibecoded design, app product/distribution, AI service business. Adopted: career pathways connected to existing Academy search; reproducible practice exercises; product and anti-generic review. Not adopted: unverified income/salary claims, arbitrary price tiers, fake social proof, unnecessary automation or decorative redesign. Career ordering is guidance, not a universal prerequisite. No data schema changes. Localization is still partial; Argos pilot is separate and must rebase before release.
